The data we may collect includes:

Primary purposes (necessary for the provision of service):

  • Resolving questions about products and services
  • Payment processing
  • Compliance with legal requirements
  • Invoicing management

Secondary purposes (service improvement — you may opt out without affecting the relationship):

  • Statistical analysis and marketing

III. With Whom Do We Share Your Information?

The company may transfer personal data to third parties without requiring your consent in the cases established by Article 37 of the Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP).

IV. How to Limit Use or Disclosure?

To limit the use of your data for advertising purposes, contact us at ventas@agama.com.mx stating your objection. We undertake to include your information in an Exclusion List to suspend all activities related to sending advertising and/or marketing, with a response within a maximum of 7 business days.

V. ARCO Rights

You have the right to Access, Rectify, Cancel or Object (ARCO) to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at ventas@agama.com.mx.

VI. National Institute of Transparency (INAI)

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may file a complaint with the INAI at www.inai.org.mx.

VII. Notice Updates

This notice may be modified due to new legal requirements, business needs or changes in our practices. Changes will be notified through www.agama.com.mx.

VIII. Acceptance

By using our services or purchasing our products, you authorize the processing of your personal data in accordance with the terms set forth in this Privacy Notice.
