Royal Blue Masterbatch MB-106 Azul Rey: Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about MB-106: compatibility, dosing, lead times, samples, and technical support.

Frequently Asked Questions about MB-106

Here are answers to the most common questions we receive about Royal Blue Masterbatch MB-106 Azul Rey. If your question is not listed, contact us via WhatsApp for a same-day response.

What shade of blue is MB-106?

MB-106 produces a vivid royal blue (similar to Pantone 286 C). The exact shade depends on resin type, wall thickness, and let-down ratio.

Can MB-106 be used in transparent applications?

MB-106 is formulated for opaque coloring. For transparent blue effects, consider AGAMA's BP-series pigment powders.

Is the color stable in outdoor conditions?

MB-106 has good lightfastness for indoor applications. For prolonged outdoor exposure, combine with UV additive AD-304.

What is the typical dosage?

1% to 4% depending on desired intensity and resin. Lower ratios produce lighter shades.

Can I get a custom blue shade?

Yes. AGAMA's formulation team can adjust the shade for production runs. Custom color matching available with a 500 kg minimum.

How does pricing compare to pigment powder?

Masterbatch costs more per kg but eliminates dust, simplifies dosing, and reduces cleaning time vs. raw pigment powder.
