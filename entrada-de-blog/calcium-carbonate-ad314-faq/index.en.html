Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about AD-314 Base Macro Batch

Calcium carbonate masterbatch used to adjust stiffness, hardness, and formulation cost in compatible plastic applications.

View productContact AGAMA

These answers summarize only the published scope for the family. When resin, equipment, or end use changes, the recommendation must be reviewed.

What is AD-314 Base Macro Batch?

AD-314 replaces part of the resin in formulations where the final performance can accommodate a mineral filler. It is not a direct drop-in replacement without validation.

Which resins can be evaluated?

Published information lists: High-density polyethylene (HDPE); Low-density polyethylene (LDPE); Linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE); Polypropylene (PP); General-purpose and impact polystyrene; Rigid and flexible PVC compounds; Solid TPU; EVA; SAN. Final compatibility must be confirmed in the actual formulation.

Which processes are documented?

Injection molding; Extrusion; Blow molding.

Which temperature reference should be used?

The product page does not publish a specific thermal window. Process within the resin range and monitor filler dispersion.

What dosage or incorporation method is recommended?

The published reference varies widely by process. Establish the percentage through stiffness, appearance, weight, and mechanical-performance trials.

Which limits should be reviewed before production?

Do not apply one generic dosage to every part. It can change shrinkage, weight, finish, and mechanical properties. It is not proposed for translucent PVC compounds.
