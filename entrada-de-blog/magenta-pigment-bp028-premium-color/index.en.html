Magenta Pigment BP-028: Premium Color for High-End Plastics Applications

At $851 MXN per kilogram, AGAMA's BP-028 is a premium magenta pigment for applications where color vibrancy, lightfastness, and heat stability justify the investment. Here is where it fits and why it costs what it does.

What makes BP-028 a premium pigment

Magenta is one of the most challenging colors to produce in pigment form. Achieving a true, vivid magenta — not pinkish-red, not violet, but that specific blue-red that sits between them — requires organic pigment chemistries that are more complex and expensive to synthesize than standard reds or blues.

BP-028 uses a high-performance organic pigment (quinacridone family) that delivers exceptional color purity, high tinting strength, and outstanding fastness properties. This is not a commodity pigment — it is an engineering-grade colorant for applications where the color must be perfect and must stay perfect.

Key specifications

  • Color: True magenta (blue-shade red, quinacridone family)
  • Form: Fine powder
  • Tinting strength: Very high (extremely low addition rates)
  • Heat stability: Excellent — rated for processing up to 300°C, suitable for engineering resins
  • Lightfastness: Excellent (blue wool scale 7-8)
  • Chemical resistance: Good resistance to solvents, acids, and alkalis
  • Compatible resins: PE, PP, PS, ABS, PA, PBT, PET, PC
  • Reference price: $851 MXN per kilogram

Why it costs more

The price of BP-028 reflects three factors:

  • Chemistry: Quinacridone pigments require multi-step synthesis with tight process control. Raw material costs are higher than simple azo reds or inorganic pigments.
  • Performance: The lightfastness, heat stability, and chemical resistance of quinacridone magenta far exceed cheaper alternatives. Parts colored with BP-028 maintain their color through years of outdoor exposure.
  • Tinting strength: Because BP-028 is so concentrated, very small amounts produce full color development. The cost per colored part is lower than the per-kilogram price suggests.
At typical addition rates of 0.1% to 0.5%, one kilogram of BP-028 colors 200 to 1,000 kilograms of finished resin. That is $0.85 to $4.25 MXN per kilogram of colored product — competitive for any application that demands this level of color quality.

Applications

BP-028 targets the high end of the plastics market:

  • Automotive exterior: Body-colored trim, mirror housings, and accent panels that must withstand years of sun exposure
  • Premium consumer goods: Cosmetic packaging, luxury household appliances, high-end electronics housings
  • Outdoor furniture: Garden chairs, loungers, and playground equipment in vibrant magenta
  • Engineering compounds: Colored PA, PBT, and PC compounds for technical applications
  • Medical devices: Color-coded components where specific shade accuracy is required

BP-028 vs. cheaper magenta alternatives

Cheaper magenta pigments exist — azo-based reds blended toward blue, for example. These can approximate the magenta shade at lower cost but fall short on:

  • Lightfastness: Azo pigments fade faster under UV, showing noticeable color shift within months of outdoor exposure
  • Heat stability: Lower-cost pigments may shift color or decompose at the processing temperatures required for engineering resins
  • Color purity: The vibrancy and chroma of a true quinacridone magenta cannot be matched by pigment blends

For applications where the magenta must be vivid and permanent, BP-028 is the correct specification. For less demanding applications, AGAMA's technical team can recommend alternatives from the broader pigments catalog.
