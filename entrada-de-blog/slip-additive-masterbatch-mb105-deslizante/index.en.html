Slip Additive Masterbatch MB-105 Deslizante: Anti-Block Performance for PE Films

AGAMA's Deslizante MB-105 is a slip and anti-block additive masterbatch designed for polyethylene film extrusion. It reduces coefficient of friction, prevents film-to-film blocking, and keeps packaging lines running at speed — all at $39 MXN per kilogram.

What MB-105 Deslizante does

In film extrusion, two problems appear the moment the polymer exits the die: the film surfaces stick to each other (blocking), and they resist sliding over machinery surfaces, rollers, and each other (high coefficient of friction, or COF). Both slow down downstream operations — bag making, form-fill-seal, and manual handling.

MB-105 is a functional additive masterbatch that addresses both issues. It contains a slip agent (typically erucamide or oleamide) and an anti-block agent (typically silica or talc) dispersed in a polyethylene carrier. When let down into the film resin at the extruder, these additives migrate to the film surface over time, creating a micro-thin layer that reduces friction and prevents adhesion between film layers.

Key specifications

  • Function: Slip (COF reduction) + anti-block
  • Active agents: Erucamide-based slip agent, mineral anti-block
  • Carrier resin: Polyethylene (LDPE/LLDPE)
  • Recommended let-down: 1% to 3% by weight
  • Compatible processes: Blown film, cast film extrusion
  • Compatible resins: LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE
  • Reference price: $39 MXN per kilogram

Applications

MB-105 is used wherever PE films need to slide, separate, and run through converting equipment without sticking:

  • Retail and grocery bags: Bags need to open easily on the rack and separate from each other
  • Stretch and shrink wrap: Controlled slip properties for pallet wrapping
  • Form-fill-seal packaging: Film must feed through FFS machines without jamming
  • Agricultural film: Mulch film and greenhouse covers that need to unroll cleanly
  • Lamination films: Base films that will be laminated to printed substrates

How slip and anti-block work together

Slip agents and anti-block agents solve different problems through different mechanisms:

  • Slip agents (amides) are migratory — they bloom to the film surface over hours, forming a lubricating molecular layer. This reduces the COF between film surfaces and between film and metal. The bloom rate depends on the specific amide, the resin type, and temperature.
  • Anti-block agents (silica, talc) are non-migratory — they create microscopic surface roughness that reduces the contact area between two film layers. This prevents the vacuum-seal effect that causes blocking, especially in wound rolls stored at warm temperatures.

Using both together in a single masterbatch like MB-105 gives the film immediate anti-block performance (from the mineral particles) plus developing slip performance (as the amide blooms over 24-72 hours after extrusion).

At $39 MXN per kilogram and typical 1-2% let-down rates, MB-105 adds less than $1 MXN per kilogram of film produced. The cost of not using it — jammed converting lines, blocked rolls, customer complaints — is significantly higher.

Combining MB-105 with color masterbatch

MB-105 is designed to be used alongside color masterbatch in the same extrusion process. Common combinations include:

  • MB-105 + MB-120 Blanco Shalom for white opaque bags and films
  • MB-105 + MB-110 Negro Kalo for black garbage bags and industrial liners
  • MB-105 + any color masterbatch from the AGAMA catalog for branded packaging films
