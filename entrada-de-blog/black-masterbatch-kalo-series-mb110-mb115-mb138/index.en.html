Black Masterbatch Kalo Series: MB-110, MB-115, and MB-138 Compared

AGAMA's Negro Kalo line offers three tiers of black masterbatch — from a cost-effective workhorse to a premium jet-black formulation. Here is how MB-110, MB-115, and MB-138 compare on performance, finish, and price so you can match the right grade to your application.

Why black masterbatch needs tiers

Black is the most widely used masterbatch color in plastics manufacturing. But not every black is the same. A drainage pipe does not need the same depth, gloss, or UV stability as an automotive interior trim. Specifying a single black grade across all applications either overpays for commodity parts or under-delivers on premium ones.

AGAMA's Negro Kalo series solves this with three formulations designed around different performance and cost targets. All three use a polyethylene carrier resin compatible with PE, PP, and most polyolefin processes, including injection molding, blown film, and extrusion.

MB-110: the economico tier

MB-110 is the entry-level black masterbatch in the Kalo family. It is formulated for applications where opacity and basic coloring matter more than surface gloss or jetness. Typical uses include:

  • Agricultural film and mulch film
  • Garbage bags and industrial liners
  • Drainage pipes and conduit
  • Non-visible structural components

At a reference price of $66 MXN per kilogram, MB-110 delivers solid carbon black dispersion at the lowest cost per part in the lineup. Recommended let-down ratios range from 2% to 5% depending on the resin and wall thickness.

MB-115: the brillante tier

MB-115 steps up in surface finish quality. The carbon black pigment used here is finer-particle, yielding better jetness and a noticeable gloss improvement over MB-110. This grade targets applications where the final part is visible to the end user:

  • Consumer packaging (caps, closures, containers)
  • Household goods and small appliances housings
  • Furniture components
  • Automotive non-structural trim

Priced at $76 MXN per kilogram, MB-115 sits in the mid-range. The cost difference over MB-110 is justified whenever surface appearance is part of the product specification. Let-down ratios of 1.5% to 4% are typical.

MB-138: the premium tier

MB-138 is the top-of-line Negro Kalo formulation. It uses a high-structure carbon black with excellent jetness, UV resistance, and dispersion uniformity. This is the grade for parts that need to look deep black under direct light and maintain that appearance over years of outdoor exposure.

  • Automotive exterior components
  • Premium consumer electronics housings
  • Outdoor furniture and playground equipment
  • High-end packaging with gloss finish requirements

At $81 MXN per kilogram, MB-138 is still competitively priced relative to imported premium blacks. The UV stability built into this formulation reduces the need for a separate UV additive in many outdoor applications, which can offset the price difference at the total-formulation level.

Side-by-side comparison

Here is how the three grades stack up on the specifications that matter most to processors:

  • Jetness: MB-110 (standard) < MB-115 (high) < MB-138 (very high)
  • Gloss: MB-110 (matte to satin) < MB-115 (satin to gloss) < MB-138 (high gloss)
  • UV stability: MB-110 (basic) < MB-115 (moderate) < MB-138 (high)
  • Dispersion: MB-110 (good) < MB-115 (very good) < MB-138 (excellent)
  • Price (MXN/kg): MB-110 ($66) < MB-115 ($76) < MB-138 ($81)
The price gap between the economy and premium tier is only $15 MXN per kilogram. At typical let-down ratios, that translates to fractions of a cent per finished part. Choose the grade based on application requirements, not just raw material cost.

Compatible processes and resins

All three Kalo grades are formulated on a PE carrier and work well in polyolefin systems. They have been validated in injection molding, blown film extrusion, pipe extrusion, and blow molding. For engineering resins or specialty carriers, AGAMA's technical team can recommend alternative formulations from the broader masterbatch catalog.
