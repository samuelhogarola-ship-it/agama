Fluorescent Green Pigment BP-065: High-Visibility Color for Safety and Signage Plastics

AGAMA's Verde Fluorescente BP-065 produces an intense, eye-catching green that glows under daylight. Designed for safety products, signage, sporting goods, and any application where visibility is the priority.

What makes BP-065 fluorescent

Fluorescent pigments work differently from standard pigments. A conventional green pigment absorbs all wavelengths except green, which it reflects. A fluorescent green pigment goes further: it absorbs ultraviolet and blue light and re-emits that energy as visible green light. The result is a color that appears to glow — it literally emits more visible green light than a non-fluorescent pigment can reflect.

BP-065 is formulated to maximize this effect in plastics. The fluorescent dye is encapsulated in a thermoset resin matrix that protects it during processing and improves its compatibility with common thermoplastic resins.

Key specifications

  • Color: Fluorescent green (daylight fluorescent)
  • Form: Fine powder
  • Fluorescence type: Daylight (no UV lamp needed — visible under normal lighting)
  • Heat stability: Up to 220-240°C (suitable for PE, PP processing; limited in high-temperature engineering resins)
  • Lightfastness: Moderate (fluorescent pigments have inherently lower lightfastness than conventional pigments)
  • Compatible resins: PE, PP, PS, ABS
  • Reference price: $429 MXN per kilogram

Applications

Fluorescent green is a specialty color used wherever maximum visibility is required:

  • Safety equipment: Hard hat accessories, safety vests and clips, traffic cones and barriers
  • Signage: Temporary construction signs, wayfinding markers, event signage
  • Sporting goods: Tennis balls, frisbees, markers, fishing lures
  • Toys and novelties: High-visibility toys, promotional items, novelty products
  • Packaging: Attention-grabbing packaging for retail shelf impact

Processing considerations

Fluorescent pigments require more care during processing than standard pigments:

  • Temperature: Keep melt temperature below 240°C. Fluorescent dyes degrade at high temperatures, causing color shift and loss of fluorescence.
  • Shear: Minimize excessive shear, which can damage the encapsulated dye particles. Standard injection and extrusion screw designs work fine.
  • Loading: Fluorescent pigments are used at higher addition rates than conventional pigments — typically 1% to 5% by weight — because the encapsulated dye particles are larger and less concentrated.
  • UV exposure: Outdoor applications will see fluorescence fade faster than indoor ones. For outdoor use, consider adding UV stabilizer AD-304 to extend the fluorescent life.
Fluorescent colors are inherently less lightfast than conventional colors. Set expectations with your end customer accordingly — fluorescent green parts are designed to be vivid and eye-catching, not permanent outdoor signage.

BP-065 vs. conventional green pigments

A standard green pigment will produce a stable, lightfast green that maintains its color for years outdoors. BP-065 produces a green that is dramatically more visible — it appears to glow — but will fade faster under UV exposure. Choose based on the application priority: durability or visibility.
