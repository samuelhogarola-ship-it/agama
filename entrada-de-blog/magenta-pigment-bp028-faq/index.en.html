Frequently asked questions about BP-028 Magenta

Opaque powder pigment for developing magenta shades in injection-molded, extruded, blow-molded, or rotationally molded parts.

View productContact AGAMA

These answers summarize only the published scope for the family. When resin, equipment, or end use changes, the recommendation must be reviewed.

What is BP-028 Magenta?

BP-028 is considered when a project requires an opaque magenta and can incorporate powder color under controlled conditions.

Which resins can be evaluated?

Published information lists: High-density polyethylene (HDPE); Low-density polyethylene (LDPE); Linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE); Polypropylene (PP); General-purpose and impact polystyrene; Rigid and flexible PVC compounds; Solid TPU; EVA. Final compatibility must be confirmed in the actual formulation.

Which processes are documented?

Cold- or hot-runner injection molding; Extrusion; Blow molding; Rotational molding.

Which temperature reference should be used?

140–210 °C is the published window; it should not be replaced with a higher generic range.

What dosage or incorporation method is recommended?

5 g/kg in injection molding; 6–8 g/kg in extrusion; 6–10 g/kg in blow molding; 7–9 g/kg in rotational molding.

Which limits should be reviewed before production?

Control dispersion and housekeeping during mixing. Approve the shade in the actual substrate. This grade is not formulated with FDA-approved materials.
