AGAMA at Plastimagen: meet us at the plastics trade show

Plastimagen brings together companies connected to the plastics industry in Mexico. AGAMA will be there as an exhibitor so visitors can meet our team in person, review samples, and discuss technical questions face to face.

What is Plastimagen?

Plastimagen is an international exhibition focused on the plastics and rubber industry in Mexico. It brings together manufacturers, converters, distributors, machinery suppliers, and material providers from across the plastics value chain.

For companies working with packaging, industrial parts, automotive components, film, profiles, and many other plastic applications, Plastimagen creates a direct space for supplier and technical conversations.

Why AGAMA is exhibiting there

AGAMA participates in Plastimagen as an exhibitor, not just as a visitor. That means our commercial and technical team is available at the stand throughout the event to speak directly with current and potential customers.

The reason is simple: the show brings together suppliers, processors, and buyers over a few days. That gives AGAMA a direct setting to discuss color consistency, dispersion, formulation, additives, and practical supply questions with the people involved in the process.

Trade shows are useful when the conversation goes beyond a brochure. Plastimagen gives visitors a place to review technical and commercial questions with the team in person.

What you can review at the AGAMA stand

If you visit AGAMA at Plastimagen, you can:

  • See physical samples of our masterbatch, pigments, and additives
  • Talk directly with the commercial and technical team
  • Discuss a specific application, resin, or process issue
  • Compare options for color, consistency, and supply continuity
  • Request samples or define a clearer follow-up after the event

You do not need to arrive with a purchase order in hand. Many productive conversations start with a real process question, a part sample, or a supplier comparison that still needs direction.

How to find us

If you plan to attend Plastimagen and want a more structured visit, the easiest path is our dedicated Plastimagen CDMX event page. There you can leave your details, define your main interest, and continue to WhatsApp with the event context already attached.

You can also stop by directly during the event. The team will be available at the stand throughout the show.

Why in-person trade show conversations still matter

One of the reasons AGAMA keeps investing in Plastimagen is that the event creates a better format for detail-heavy conversations. In plastics, small variables matter: loading percentage, base resin, process temperature, dispersion behaviour, and end-use expectations.

Those conversations often move faster in person than by email. A sample on the table, a visible defect, or a simple production question can turn into a much clearer next step when both sides share the same context.

If you want to arrive with that context already framed, you can book your stand visit from the event page.

If you also want to see how AGAMA is structuring lead capture, follow-up, and content around this kind of event, you can review our B2B trade show plan for industrial fairs in Mexico.

Can’t attend this year?

If you cannot make it to Plastimagen, AGAMA still supports customers across Mexico through our branch network, direct WhatsApp contact, and AGAMA Online. You can still request information, compare products, and continue the conversation remotely.

See you at Plastimagen.
