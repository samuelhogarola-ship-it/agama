Royal Blue Masterbatch MB-106 Azul Rey: Vivid, Consistent Blue for Plastics

AGAMA's Azul Rey MB-106 delivers a deep royal blue with high tinting strength and excellent dispersion. Formulated in Mexico for polyolefin injection, extrusion, and blow molding applications.

What MB-106 Azul Rey is

MB-106 is a concentrated royal blue masterbatch formulated on a polyethylene carrier resin. The pigment system delivers a rich, deep blue — what the industry refers to as ultramarine or royal blue — with high tinting strength that keeps let-down ratios low and per-part coloring cost manageable.

AGAMA manufactures MB-106 in-house under the trade name Azul Rey. The formulation is optimized for clean dispersion across a range of polyolefin processes, from high-speed injection molding to blown film extrusion.

Key specifications

  • Color: Royal blue (ultramarine family)
  • Carrier resin: Polyethylene
  • Recommended let-down: 1% to 4% by weight
  • Compatible processes: Injection molding, blown film, cast film, pipe extrusion, blow molding
  • Compatible resins: PE, PP, and most polyolefins
  • Reference price: $149 MXN per kilogram

Where MB-106 is used

Royal blue is a high-demand color in both consumer and industrial plastics. Common applications for MB-106 include:

  • Consumer packaging: Caps, closures, and containers for household chemicals, personal care, and food products
  • Crates and bins: Returnable transport packaging, storage bins, and industrial totes
  • Household goods: Buckets, basins, chairs, and storage solutions
  • Automotive: Interior trim components, under-hood parts, and non-visible structural elements
  • Agricultural: Blue irrigation pipe, water tanks, and horticultural containers

Color strength and consistency

Blue pigments vary widely in tinting strength. A weak blue concentrate forces higher addition rates, which increases cost per part and can affect the mechanical properties of the base resin. MB-106 is formulated for high tinting strength — most applications achieve full color development at 2% to 3% let-down.

Batch-to-batch consistency is controlled through spectrophotometric measurement at AGAMA's lab. Each production lot is checked against a master standard before release, ensuring that repeat orders match previous shipments within tight color tolerance.

For critical color-match applications, request a pre-production sample from the specific lot. AGAMA can ship lab samples within days for approval before releasing the full order.

MB-106 vs. BP-080 pigment powder

AGAMA also offers Azul Rey BP-080, a royal blue pigment in powder form. The choice between masterbatch and powder depends on your process:

  • Masterbatch (MB-106): Pre-dispersed in a carrier resin. Easier to handle, meter, and mix. Less dust. Ideal for processors who do not have their own compounding line.
  • Pigment powder (BP-080): Higher pigment concentration. Lower cost per unit of color. Requires a compounding step or dry-blending expertise. Best for compounders and large-volume processors with twin-screw equipment.

If you are unsure which format fits your process, AGAMA's technical team can advise based on your equipment and volumes.
