AGAMA at Meximold: find us at booth 750

Meximold brings together companies and suppliers involved in plastics moulding and processing in Mexico. AGAMA is at booth 750 with pigments, masterbatch, and additives so you can review samples and questions directly with our team.

What is Meximold?

Meximold is a specialised meeting point for manufacturers, converters, engineers, buyers, and suppliers connected to plastics processing and moulding in Mexico.

For many companies, that creates a direct setting to review materials, color issues, dispersion questions, resin compatibility, and supply alternatives with people who understand how the process works in practice.

Why AGAMA exhibits at Meximold

AGAMA goes to Meximold because it matches an important part of our day-to-day work: speaking with plastics manufacturers who need faster, more technical guidance. The goal is not only to show products, but to understand what is happening in the plant and define a better next step.

If your team is reviewing formulations, comparing suppliers, improving dispersion, or trying to stabilise color and process performance, Meximold gives you a direct setting to meet AGAMA's commercial and technical team in the same place.

A useful event conversation starts with a real process question. Meximold gives visitors a way to review samples, technical doubts, and next steps with shared context.

What you can review with AGAMA at the stand

If you visit AGAMA at Meximold, you can:

  • Review options in pigments, masterbatch, and additives based on your application
  • Talk about resin compatibility, formulation decisions, and process constraints
  • Compare alternatives to improve dispersion, consistency, and supply continuity
  • Bring a real sample, part, or production issue to guide the follow-up more clearly
  • Define whether the right next step is testing, quotation, or a deeper technical review

You do not need to arrive with everything decided. Many of the best trade show conversations start with a narrow but important question: why the color changed, which concentrate fits better, how a mineral load is affecting the part, or how to compare a new supplier more accurately.

How to find us: booth 750

Look for booth 750 on the venue map — that is where the AGAMA team will be for the entire show. If you want to make sure the right person is available when you stop by, send us a WhatsApp message beforehand and we will coordinate the timing.

Walk-ins are also welcome. The team is at booth 750 throughout the event with no appointment required.

What makes an event conversation worth it

Meximold is most useful when it shortens decisions that would otherwise drag through email. In plastics, small changes in pigment loading, additives, resin compatibility, or dispersion can significantly affect the final result. In-person conversations make those questions easier to frame.

If you are evaluating a supplier change, comparing masterbatch options, refining a color, or looking for more responsive technical support, the trade show is the right moment to move that decision forward. Write us on WhatsApp if you want to arrive with the topic already framed.

Can’t attend this year?

If you cannot make it to Queretaro this year, AGAMA still supports customers through our branch network in Mexico, AGAMA Online, and direct WhatsApp contact. The event helps, but it is not the only way to start a productive conversation.
