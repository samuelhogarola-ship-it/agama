Visit objective

Arrive with a clear objective

Plastimagen is useful when you leave with a clearer decision: what material to test, what data is missing for a quote, or what color/additive should be manufactured. AGAMA does not need a perfect brief; it needs the real case to guide the first trial.

  • Identify whether you need powder pigment, standard masterbatch or color development.
  • Review the process: injection, extrusion, blow molding, film, packaging or a technical part.
  • Detect whether the issue is shade, opacity, dispersion, release, UV, moisture or supply.
  • Define the next step: catalog, sample, quote or custom manufacturing/development.
