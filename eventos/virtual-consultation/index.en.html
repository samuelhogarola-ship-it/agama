Technical conversation

Request a virtual consultation with AGAMA

Discuss resin, process, color, additive, sample, and supply questions with AGAMA before selecting a product or planning a trial.

Contact AGAMA

A virtual consultation gives purchasing, production, and development teams a structured first conversation before selecting a pigment, masterbatch, or additive.

Topics suited to a virtual meeting

  • Clarifying the resin and conversion process
  • Reviewing a color reference or existing product code
  • Understanding documented compatibility and restrictions
  • Planning a sample or production-representative trial
  • Discussing order channel, destination, and packaging needs

What to send in advance

Share the company, participants, preferred time zone, polymer, process, application, color reference, and the question you need to resolve. Avoid sending confidential formulations unless an appropriate agreement is already in place.

What the meeting does not replace

A conversation cannot approve a finished part, determine regulatory compliance, or guarantee performance without the relevant documents and testing. Project-specific conclusions remain subject to validation.

How scheduling works

Send a request through AGAMA's contact channel. The team will review the topic, identify the appropriate participant, and confirm an available meeting time.

Scheduling details

Send the resin, process, application, target color or problem, time zone, and the decision you need to make after the call.
