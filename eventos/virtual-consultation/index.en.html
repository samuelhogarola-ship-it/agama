Consultation agenda

What gets covered in a virtual consultation

Every call is structured around your specific case. AGAMA's team reviews your application, recommends products and outlines next steps -- all in 15 to 30 minutes.

  • Color matching and development -- share a Pantone, RAL, physical sample or photo and discuss how AGAMA approaches the match for your specific resin and process.
  • Resin compatibility -- review which pigments, masterbatch or additives work with your polymer (PP, PE, PET, PVC, ABS, etc.) and process (injection, extrusion, blow molding, film).
  • Product recommendations -- get specific catalog codes or custom formulation options based on your application, end use and performance requirements.
  • Pricing and sampling -- discuss pricing structure, minimum order quantities, lead times and how to request physical samples shipped to your location.
  • Supply and logistics -- review shipping options from Mexico to the US or other markets, packaging, labeling and supply continuity for regular orders.

Consultations happen via Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams -- your choice. A call link is sent after the appointment is confirmed via WhatsApp.

Before the call

What to prepare for the best results

The more context you share in advance, the more productive the consultation. Send details via WhatsApp or include them in the form below so the team can prepare.

  • Color references -- Pantone, RAL, a photo of the target shade, or a description of the color you need.
  • Resin and process -- the polymer you use and the manufacturing process (injection, extrusion, blow molding, film, etc.).
  • End application -- what you are making (packaging, automotive part, consumer product, industrial component, etc.).
  • Volume and timeline -- estimated quantities and any production deadlines help AGAMA prepare pricing and lead time information.
  • Current challenges -- if you are troubleshooting color variation, dispersion, supplier reliability or formulation issues, share the details for a focused discussion.
