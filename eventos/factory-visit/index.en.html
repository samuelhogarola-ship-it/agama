Tour overview

What you see during the visit

A factory visit at AGAMA is structured around four areas. The goal is to give buyers and technical teams a clear picture of how pigments and masterbatch are formulated, tested, manufactured and stored before shipment.

  • Formulation lab -- where color development starts. See how AGAMA matches target shades, tests resin compatibility and develops custom formulations for injection, extrusion, blow molding and film.
  • QC lab with spectrophotometer -- where every batch is tested against the approved standard. Review how color tolerance, dispersion and consistency are measured before production release.
  • Production lines -- see extrusion, compounding and pelletizing equipment in operation. Understand capacity, batch sizes and how AGAMA handles standard and custom manufacturing runs.
  • Warehouse and logistics -- review packaging, labeling, inventory management and shipping preparation. Relevant for buyers planning regular supply from Mexico to the US or other markets.

AGAMA's facility is in the Mexico City metropolitan area, accessible from Mexico City International Airport (MEX). The team provides directions and can help coordinate logistics for international visitors.

Prepare for your visit

What to bring for the best results

The more context you share in advance, the more productive the visit. AGAMA's technical team prepares materials, samples and data based on what you send before arriving.

  • Physical samples or target colors -- a molded part, Pantone/RAL reference, approved sample or even a photo of the shade you need.
  • Resin and process details -- the polymer (PP, PE, PET, PVC, ABS, etc.), the process (injection, extrusion, blow molding, film) and the end application.
  • Volume and timeline -- estimated quantities, purchase frequency and any production deadlines help AGAMA prepare catalog options or custom manufacturing proposals.
  • Current challenges -- if you are switching suppliers, troubleshooting color variation, dispersion issues or process problems, bring the data and the team will review it on-site.
