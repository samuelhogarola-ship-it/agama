frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about plastics color

Do you manufacture masterbatch or pigments with FDA-approved materials?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. At Agama, we can develop both pigments and masterbatch using raw materials certified by the FDA (Food and Drug Administration).

These products are ideal for applications that require regulatory compliance for contact with food, beverages, or pharmaceutical products.

It is important to mention that the availability of colors with FDA certification is limited, since it depends on the approvals of the raw materials, which mainly come from Europe and, to a lesser extent, Asia.

If you would like more information or personalized advice, feel free to contact us.

Do you offer any purge compound for PET?
arrow_forward_ios

No. We currently do not offer a purge compound compatible with PET.

Recommendation: We suggest checking specialized purge-compound manufacturers such as UltraPurge, AsaClean, and ChemTrend.

Can MB-105 Slip Agent be used as a color?
arrow_forward_ios

No. MB-105 Slip Agent is not a color; it is a masterbatch additive designed to improve slip and anti-block properties during plastic film production. It does not add color or visual effect.

Is MB-120 white masterbatch made from polyethylene?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. MB-120 Blanco Shalom is a polyethylene-based white masterbatch. It is compatible with PE, PP, crystal polystyrene (GPPS), HIPS, MIPS, rigid and flexible PVC compounds, and some engineering resins.

How do I place an online order with AGAMA Online?
arrow_forward_ios

The process is simple: choose your products from the catalogue, send us your list via WhatsApp or fill in the contact form with quantities, and we will send you a quote within 24 business hours.

Once the order is confirmed, you can pay by bank transfer, card, or cash. We coordinate shipping to anywhere in Mexico.
