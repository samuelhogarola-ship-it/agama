frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about plastics color

Do you manufacture masterbatch or pigments with FDA-approved materials?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. At Agama, we can develop both pigments and masterbatch using raw materials certified by the FDA (Food and Drug Administration).

These products are ideal for applications that require regulatory compliance for contact with food, beverages, or pharmaceutical products.

It is important to mention that the availability of colors with FDA certification is limited, since it depends on the approvals of the raw materials, which mainly come from Europe and, to a lesser extent, Asia.

If you would like more information or personalized advice, feel free to contact us.

Do you offer any purge compound for PET?
arrow_forward_ios

No, unfortunately. We currently do not have a purge compound compatible with PET.

Recommendation: We suggest consulting manufacturers specialized in purge compounds such as UltraPurge, AsaClean, and ChemTrend.

Can MB-105 Slip be used as a color?
arrow_forward_ios

No. MB-105 Slip is not a color, but rather an additive in masterbatch form, designed to improve slip and antiblock properties during plastic film manufacturing. It does not add color or visual effect.

Is white masterbatch MB-120 made from polyethylene?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. White masterbatch MB-120 Blanco Shalom is a polyethylene-based masterbatch. It is compatible with PE, PP, crystal polystyrene (GPPS), HIPS, MIPS, rigid and flexible PVC compounds, and some engineering resins.
