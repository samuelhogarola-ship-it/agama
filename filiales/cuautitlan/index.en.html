Todo lo que necesitas saber sobre color plástico

¿Fabrican masterbatch o pigmentos con materiales aprobados por la FDA?
arrow_forward_ios

Sí, en Agama podemos desarrollar tanto pigmentos como masterbatch utilizando materias primas certificadas por la FDA (Food and Drug Administration).

Estos productos son ideales para aplicaciones donde se requiere cumplimiento normativo en contacto con alimentos, bebidas o productos farmacéuticos.

Es importante mencionar que la disponibilidad de colores con certificación FDA es limitada, ya que depende de las aprobaciones de las materias primas, que son principalmente de origen europeo y, en menor medida, de Asia.

Si deseas más información o asesoría personalizada, no dudes en contactarnos.

Do you offer any purge compound for PET?
arrow_forward_ios

No. We currently do not offer a purge compound compatible with PET.

Recommendation: We suggest checking specialized purge-compound manufacturers such as UltraPurge, AsaClean, and ChemTrend.

Can MB-105 Slip Agent be used as a color?
arrow_forward_ios

No. MB-105 Slip Agent is not a color; it is a masterbatch additive designed to improve slip and anti-block properties during plastic film production. It does not add color or visual effect.

Is MB-120 white masterbatch made from polyethylene?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. MB-120 Blanco Shalom is a polyethylene-based white masterbatch. It is compatible with PE, PP, crystal polystyrene (GPPS), HIPS, MIPS, rigid and flexible PVC compounds, and some engineering resins.

Do you handle deliveries and pickups in Cuautitlan?
arrow_forward_ios

Yes. Our Cuautitlan branch can help you with product availability, pickups, and deliveries in the area. Contact us through the form or WhatsApp and we will confirm the best option for your order.
